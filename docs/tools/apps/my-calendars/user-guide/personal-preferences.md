# Personal Preferences

Use Calendar Settings to adjust your view and optional sources.
The administrator decides which settings you can change.

![Personal calendar settings and timeline preferences](/img/tools/my-calendars/personal-settings.png)

## Change Your View And Timeline

Choose a start time, visible hours, and whether to show weekends.
Choose the administrator default to use the shared setting.
The administrator sets the time grid for Day and Week views.

Choose a view from the toolbar: **Day**, **Week**, or **Month**.
That choice is saved with your personal settings.

## Change A Source

You may be able to change a source's name, color, or logo.
Administrators can allow or block each supported change.
You cannot change SharePoint field mappings for an assigned source.
You cannot hide or remove a Mandatory source.

## Save Or Reset

Select **Save** to keep your changes.
Select **Cancel** to discard edits in the open panel.
Select **Reset to Defaults** to clear personal choices.
This restores the available administrator values.

Personal preferences are stored in your OneDrive app folder.
They follow your account across sites that use My Calendars.
