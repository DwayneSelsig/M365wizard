# Apps In Microsoft Teams

**An app is a tool used to get the work done.** Choose it for the task or information the collaboration needs. Teams can make that tool available in a personal view, within a chat or channel, or during a conversation or meeting. An app does not have to be a channel tab.

## Choose The Right Context

| Context | What it gives people | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Personal app | A view of a person's work across conversations and Teams, available through the Teams app bar when added or pinned. | Planner can show a person's tasks and plans. |
| Chat or channel | A shared entry point for the people working together on a topic. | Add a relevant plan or dashboard as a tab where the group discusses it. |
| Message or meeting | A tool used at the moment of a conversation, such as an action in the compose area or an app in a meeting. | Start an approval from a chat or bring an app into a meeting when supported. |

Microsoft describes [personal apps, chat and channel tabs, meeting apps, and messaging actions](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/teams/apps-service/add-an-app-to-microsoft-teams). These are capabilities of individual apps, not features every app provides. The [Planner app in Teams](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/planner/teams/getting-started-with-planner-in-teams), for example, offers a personal view of tasks and plans. A tab in a channel can instead point people to a plan used for that topic.

## Keep The Tool Connected To Its Work

Use Planner for tasks, Lists for tracked records, or Power BI for a dashboard when those tools fit the work. Add an app to a channel only when the people in that topic need it there. A personal app can be useful without appearing in any Team; an app in a meeting may serve only that meeting. Microsoft also documents [starting an approval from a Teams chat](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/teams/shifts/create-an-approval-from-a-chat).

Teams can present a connected service while the underlying information remains with that service. For example, channel documents are managed in SharePoint. A Power BI report added to Teams still needs [Power BI permissions](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-bi/collaborate-share/service-collaborate-microsoft-teams). Adding an entry point in Teams does not grant people access to every source behind it. See [Teams and SharePoint integration](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/sharepoint/teams-connected-sites).

:::warning[Check Access And Ownership]

Before adding an app for a group, check who owns its data, who can use it, and who will maintain it. Team owners and IT may restrict app installation or availability through Teams settings and policies. See [manage access to Teams apps](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/app-policies).

:::
