## One place to continue the work

Home brings Chat and Cowork together. Office in Copilot adds editable Word documents, Excel workbooks, and PowerPoint presentations to the conversation. Microsoft's [September 25, 2026 announcement](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2026/09/25/introducing-the-new-copilot-with-home-code-and-autopilot/) describes the combined experience and its rollout.

## Four ways to use Copilot

The video's four modes help explain what you hand over:

| Mode | What you ask Copilot to do |
| --- | --- |
| [Assist: Chat](../docs/services/copilot/assist) | Answer a question, draft text, or help analyze information. |
| [Delegate: Cowork](../docs/services/copilot/delegate) | Carry out a multistep task and return a result to review. |
| [Build: Code](../docs/services/copilot/code) | Turn a description into a working tool, such as a tracker or dashboard. |
| [Automate: Autopilot](../docs/services/copilot/automate) | Keep working on an ongoing responsibility within agreed boundaries. |

Microsoft describes [Autopilot as a cloud-hosted agent with its own identity](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2026/09/25/introducing-the-new-copilot-with-home-code-and-autopilot/). Giving it an ongoing responsibility also means agreeing who checks its actions and when it must ask for approval.

:::info[Check preview access and billing]

Microsoft Learn describes [Code](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/modules/build-solutions-copilot-code/) and [Autopilot](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/modules/delegate-ongoing-work-autopilot-copilot/) as Frontier preview experiences. For organizational use, [Cowork, Code, and Autopilot use Copilot Credits through usage-based billing](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/user-subscription-license-usage-based-billing), with a required user subscription license and an administrator-configured billing policy. An announcement does not guarantee access in your account.

:::

## Credits and cost control

Credits measure eligible AI consumption. [Funding options include pay-as-you-go, monthly capacity packs, and pre-purchase plans](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/usage-based-billing-compare-dashboard-views). The available credit sources and policy settings determine how usage is charged.

I would measure representative pilot tasks before committing to capacity. Have the administrator configure spending policies, limits, and alerts using [Microsoft's cost management guidance](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/usage-based-billing-manage-copilot-credits).

## Start with one task you can check

Choose a task whose result you can judge, using information with a clear owner. Agree who reviews the output. If you build a tool, assign someone to maintain it before colleagues depend on it. Expand when the result is useful and the review effort is manageable.

Use the [Copilot overview](../docs/services/copilot) to choose a starting point and [Licensing And Costs](../docs/services/copilot/licensing-and-costs) to discuss access and funding.
